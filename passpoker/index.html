Rules as built
  • Each player gets two 5-card hands (A and B) and 10 bid cards: 0–4 with ‖, and 0–4 with ✕.
  • Each round has 3 passes. For each pass, both players secretly put a bid card on each hand. Bid cards used in a round don't come back until the next round.
  • Even split: the 4 numbers are added up and divided by 4, then rounded to the nearest whole number (.5 rounds down). Every hand passes that many cards.
  • ‖ sends straight across to the mirror hand (your A to their A). ✕ crosses over (your A to their B).
  • Split by your bids: each player passes half the bid total (.5 rounds down). That's split between your two hands in proportion to your own two bids (4 and 1 with 3 to pass → 2 and 1). On an exact half, the hand with the higher bid passes the extra card; with equal bids, the ✕ hand does. A hand passes at most 5, and any extra comes from the other hand.
  • Original: the passes always go ✕, then ‖, then ✕, and bid cards are ● or ▲ instead. Each of your hands swaps with one of theirs, and both pass the average of those two bids. An average ending in .5 rounds down if the two cards share a symbol, and up if they differ.
  • If a hand ends a pass with more than 5 cards, its owner chooses 5 to keep there in secret. The rest go to their other hand.
  • After 3 passes there are 3 matchups: high hand vs high hand, low vs low, and best 5 of all 10 cards vs best 5 of all 10.
  • On a tie, the top card of the deck is flipped and added to both hands. If it's still tied, a coin flip decides.
  • For each matchup, the winner picks a card from that matchup's 10 cards, then the loser picks. After the round, each player keeps 3 cards.
  • Spoils: passing works like Even split. In each matchup's draft the winner picks, then the loser, then the winner again, so you can end with 6 to 12 cards. The best 5 wins; if those tie, compare the leftover cards from highest down.
  • Offer one extra (3rd pass only): each hand that passes cards offers one more than it has to. The receiver keeps the right number and sends one back, either to the hand it came from or to the passer's other hand, depending on the setting. With "other hand", that's a sneaky way to move a card between your own hands, but the receiver decides which card goes back.
  • Swaps: after passes 1 and 2, each player may trade one card from their A with one from their B. An open swap costs 1 bid card, and both moved cards are shown to the opponent. A secret swap costs 2, and the opponent only learns that you swapped. You can't spend cards you'll need for the passes still to come.
  • First-pick auction: each round you have 4 bid cards left after passing. Before each matchup's draft, both players secretly play one, and the lower number picks first (a tie goes to the matchup winner). In Spoils, the matchup winner still gets two cards. Spending big bids while passing saves your low cards for the draft.
  • Round 2 is played the same way, with a fresh deck that leaves out the kept cards. The best 5 of your 6 kept cards wins. If those tie, the higher 6th card wins.
